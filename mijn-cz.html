Privacy & preferences

You decide how CZ contacts you and what we may do with your data. Changes apply across the website, the app, email and our contact centre.

How we may contact you

Messages about your policy, invoices and claims are always sent — we are legally required to reach you about those.

Policy and service messages Renewals, invoices, claim decisions. Cannot be switched off.
EmailHealth tips, member offers and the CZ newsletter.
Text messageShort reminders, for example about an expiring reimbursement.
Push notificationsAlerts in the CZ app when a claim is settled.
TelephoneOur care advisors may call you about your cover.
PostPrinted policy documents and the annual overview.

How often you hear from us

Rather than switching everything off, you can turn the volume down.

What you want to hear about

Pick the subjects that fit your situation. We will leave the rest out.

Use of your health data

Health data is sensitive, so we only use it beyond claim handling if you say yes.

These choices are recorded separately with the wording of the notice you were shown, and can be withdrawn at any moment without affecting your cover.

Personal care advice based on my claims Lets an advisor see, for example, that you are mid-treatment and suggest a contracted clinic with a shorter wait.
Invitations to prevention programmes We may approach you about programmes such as stop-smoking support or back-pain coaching.
Anonymised use for healthcare research Aggregated, non-identifiable data helps us see where care in your region falls short.
